About the System

Our E-Voting System is designed to modernize the electoral process with advanced CNIC verification and facial biometrics. Every voter's identity is verified through their national ID card and live face matching, ensuring that every vote counts and preventing duplicate registrations. With AES-256 encryption and comprehensive security measures, we guarantee the integrity and anonymity of your choice.

Benefits

  • Secure: CNIC verification with facial biometrics.
  • Fast: Cast your vote in seconds.
  • Transparent: Real-time results and audit trails.
  • Accessible: Vote from anywhere with ease.

Requirements

To participate, you will need:

  • Valid CNIC Number.
  • CNIC Image (Clear photo of your CNIC card).
  • Mobile Number for registration.
  • Age 18+ (Verified from CNIC).
  • Face Verification (Camera required).
